Lift Out

Lift any block out of a page, get a plain-language description, and see what it relates to.

Stored only in this browser, in the extension's own storage (not encrypted, not synced). It is sent only to api.anthropic.com.

How to use it

  • Mouse: hold Alt (Option ⌥ on a Mac) and move over a page to see the outline, then click.
  • Keyboard: press Alt+Shift+L (Option+Shift+L on a Mac, or click the toolbar icon), move with ↑ ↓, press Enter to lift.
  • Esc puts the block back.
  • Boss key: Alt+Shift+B (Option+Shift+B on a Mac) shows a page that explains Lift Out; press it again to hide it.

What gets sent

When you lift a block, its text, up to three images or charts inside it, the page title and address, short excerpts of other blocks on the page, and small copies of up to eight other images on the page are sent to Anthropic's API to write the description and choose related items. The extension downloads those images itself to do this. Nothing is sent until you lift something.
