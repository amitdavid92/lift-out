A Chrome extension for accessibility

Lift Out

Click any part of a web page to pull it out of the noise, have it explained in plain language, and see what it connects to.

Businesses in the area pay an extra charge, and there is a cap on building costs.
  • LiftThe block leaves the page for a clean, enlarged, high-contrast panel. Adverts and sidebars stay behind.
  • DescribeA plain-language explanation streams in beside it, for text, images and charts. One click shortens it to a sentence.
  • RelateCards fan out with the table, footnote or picture it refers to. Click one to swap it in.

The one takeaway

Understanding a page shouldn't depend on how well you can fight it.

Hold Alt (Option on a Mac) and click a block to try it. Press Esc or the same shortcut again to close this page.
